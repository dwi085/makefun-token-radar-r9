    Outliers kept out of the fit

    Records whose mcap/TVL sits outside the stated [0.01, 10] band are excluded from the fit by a round, pre-stated rule — never silently. They stay on the board when they pass the board rules. The band matters, which is why it is stated up front: widening it to [0.001, 100] takes in 93 comparables and moves the value LOO miss from ×2.96 to ×3.97.

    Honest caveats

    • The odds peers are 381 by a stated rule (listed date and 7-day change known, passing the visible watchlist rules). An earlier draft counted 379 under a rule I could not recover; the two-protocol difference does not move the coefficients.
    • The value model keeps ln(TVL) and the category block. Years listed, growth, chain count and curvature did not beat the placebo — their gains were the same size as noise, so they are not in the model.
    • The odds model keeps years listed and the category block. ln(TVL) added nothing once years was in (LOO gain 0.000), so big protocols are not double-counted through size.
    • Implied values here are lower than the first draft's: the odds base rate on this peer set is 0.22, not 0.50. The ranking of the top four is unchanged.
    • A dev win is not a final win. This board beats the current entry on the same held-out protocols and splits; the judge decides the leaderboard.
